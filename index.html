The novel behind the moon

Snowmoon is Vitalik Buterin's sci-fi novel — cities that vote on everything, cameras that never blink, and people who still choose privacy. This site and token are a fan tribute to that story.

"In Veridia, the moon is a reminder: some light should stay cold, and some lives should stay unwatched."

Gladias climbs the tower stairs. Seila keeps the Order of Steering honest. Zei and Bai move through rain-soaked streets. Emerald AI watches the Watch. We built $SNOWMOON for readers who loved that ice-and-neon world.
